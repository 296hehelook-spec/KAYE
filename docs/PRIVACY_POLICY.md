# Kaye AlumniFinder - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 12, 2026*
*Governed in alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).*

---

### 1. Fundamental Principle: Consent and Minimization
Kaye is designed exclusively to facilitate respectful, academic, and career networking between students and college alumni. We believe in absolute data minimization and user agency.

### 2. Information We Display
We display only publicly indexed professional information retrieved via public search engines or directly provided by self-registered alumni:
- Full Name
- Professional Headline
- Stated Current Role & Company
- City/Geographic Region
- College, Graduation Year, and Department
- Direct Link to Public LinkedIn Profile

### 3. Information We Explicitly DO NOT Collect
Under no circumstances does Kaye collect, guess, derive, predict, or display:
- Personal or work email addresses
- Personal or telephone numbers
- Home addresses or financial records
- Breached or leaked credential datasets
- OSINT pattern-guessed mailboxes

### 4. How Connection Requests Work
To prevent unwanted solicitation:
1. Students cannot send in-app messages to alumni who have not explicitly self-registered on Kaye.
2. For agent-discovered public profiles, students are directed to view the public profile on LinkedIn and connect through LinkedIn's native consent mechanisms.
3. For self-registered alumni, connection requests require a respectful pledge, are limited to 5 per day per student, and only reveal contact details if the alumnus affirmatively accepts the request.

### 5. Immediate Right to Opt-Out & Erasure
Any individual whose profile appears on Kaye may submit their public LinkedIn URL on our **Opt-Out Page** (`/opt-out`) or by clicking the "Remove my profile" link on any result card.
- Opt-outs take effect **immediately**.
- The profile is permanently added to an exclusion ledger and purged from all search caches.
- Future search agent operations automatically discard the profile prior to returning results.

### 6. Disclaimer of Affiliation
Kaye is an independent educational tool. Kaye is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation, Microsoft, or any academic institution listed.
